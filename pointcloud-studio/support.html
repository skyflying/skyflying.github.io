PointCloud Studio for iPad and Mac

Support

For questions, bug reports and suggestions, write to smingyi.hsu@gmail.com. Please include the app version (shown under Help › About PointCloud Studio), your device and system version, and — if possible — the steps or a small sample file that shows the problem.

Requirements

  • iPad with iPadOS 17 or later, or a Mac with macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon or Intel).
  • Large point clouds and photogrammetry benefit from more memory; recent iPad Pro / M-series Macs are recommended.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I open?
Point clouds in LAS, LAZ and XYZ / text formats; GeoTIFF and ESRI ASCII grids; routes and vector files (Shapefile, GeoPackage, GeoJSON, KML, GPX, DXF, CSV); and PointCloud Studio projects.

Are projects compatible with the Windows version?
Yes. Projects saved on iPad or Mac open in PointCloud Studio for Windows and the other way round.

What is free and what do Pro and Advanced add?
Viewing, cleaning, TIN / DEM surfaces, sections and exports are free. Pro adds coordinate systems, vector files, routes, filters and classification, contours, volumes, earthworks and cable analyses. Advanced adds hydrographic QC (CUBE, IHO S-44), multi-year cable assessment, scour and sand waves, land analyses, meshes and models, and point clouds from photos, video or a stereo rig. The bundled sample photos can be reconstructed for free (Reconstruct › Try with Sample Photos…).

I bought Pro / Advanced on another device. How do I unlock it here?
Open Help › Upgrade to Pro / Advanced and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription?
In the App Store: your account › Subscriptions, or on the Mac in System Settings › your name › Media & Purchases. Lifetime purchases are not subscriptions and do not renew.

Can the results be used for navigation?
No. Results are for engineering reference only and do not replace certified hydrographic software and QC procedures.
