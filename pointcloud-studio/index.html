iPad & Mac

PointCloud Studio for iPad and Mac

The core tools are free; Pro and Advanced are in-app purchases. Processing stays on the device.

iPadOS 17 · macOS 14 | Swift · Metal

Free

  • • LAS, LAZ and XYZ point clouds; GeoTIFF / ASCII grids
  • • Fast 3D view, colour by height, intensity, RGB or class
  • • Box, polygon and section selection; reject and trash
  • • TIN and DEM surfaces (incl. shoalest depth), sections
  • • Export points, TIN and DEM; Windows-compatible projects

Pro

  • • Coordinate systems (EPSG), vector files, routes and KP
  • • Noise filters; ground, vegetation and building classes
  • • Subset editor, contours, volumes, earthworks design
  • • Depth of burial, free spans, rock berms, structures

Advanced

  • • Hydrographic QC: CUBE and IHO S-44 Ed. 6
  • • Multi-year cable assessment with Excel report and figures
  • • Scour at monopiles and sand wave migration
  • • Land: terrain, change detection, stockpiles, roads, hydrology
  • • Meshes and models (planes, cylinders, buildings)
  • • Point clouds from photos, video or a stereo rig

Try photogrammetry for free

  • • Reconstruct › Try with Sample Photos…
  • • 12 bundled aerial photos with ground control points
  • • Your own photos, video and stereo rigs: Advanced

Your data stays on the device

  • • No account, analytics, advertising or tracking
  • • No uploads — the app makes no network requests
  • • Purchases are handled by Apple

Requirements

  • • iPad with iPadOS 17 or later
  • • Mac with macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon or Intel)
  • • More memory helps with very large clouds and photos
