PointCloud Studio for iPad and Mac

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

This policy describes how the PointCloud Studio app for iPadOS and macOS ("the app"), developed by Mingyi Hsu, handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data the app collects

None. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Your files

Point clouds, surfaces, routes, vector files, photos, videos and projects that you open are processed only on your device. The app reads and writes only the files and folders you choose (and its own app folder). It does not upload them. Location information stored inside your own files — for example GPS positions in the EXIF data of photos used to build a point cloud — is read on the device only to georeference that result.

Network

The app does not need an internet connection and makes no network requests of its own. The only exception is the purchase process described below, which is handled by Apple.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app only receives from Apple whether a purchase is active, so that the corresponding features can be unlocked on your devices. See Apple's privacy policy.

Settings stored on your device

The app keeps your preferences (for example display settings and recently opened projects) on your device using the standard system storage. They are not transmitted anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
