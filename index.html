About Mingyi

Hello, I am Mingyi (Ming-Yi) Hsu, a Subsea Engineer and Geospatial Specialist with deep experience across marine spatial infrastructure, renewable energy development, and software automation.

My career bridges foundational public sector leadership with global offshore energy engineering. At the Ministry of the Interior, I led initiatives within Taiwan’s national marine survey program, directed the compilation of official national electronic navigational charts (ENC) and marine base maps, and developed national-scale spatial analytics and terrain models. Today, I translate that large-scale geospatial foundation into managing spatial data integrity, engineering subsea infrastructure, and developing automated desktop analysis tools for offshore wind and marine installations.

From regional terrain evolution and seabed stability to linear infrastructure clearance and land-based earthworks for renewables, my work delivers mathematical precision, clean engineering workflows, and practical decision-making tools.
